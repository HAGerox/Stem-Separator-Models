## Evidence and recommendations

Fourteen exact displayed benchmark rows are retained from seven identifiable submissions: 10837, 10838, 10845, 10846, 10847, 10848 and 10930. Source URLs identify the exact entry. Original Anvuew and Gilliaan Karaoke checkpoint LFS hashes were matched against the admitted registry hashes. The SW submission uses a source-defined merged `other` and residual instrumental; those transformations are recorded in its config.

Each new submission has a separate benchmark suite because its inference settings, output processing, or unreported protocol do not establish equivalence to older records. This prevents `rank.py` from mechanically comparing them with unrelated configurations. Full historical rows remain intact.

The guide explicitly reports Revive 3e as fuller and noisier than Big Beta 5e. Two ordinal observations preserve that tradeoff, tied to the current hashed export, lines 7767–7773. Neither compatibility nor this comparison establishes a replacement for the incumbent.

Ran `scripts/rank.py` for vocals, instrumental, drums, lead vocals, backing instrumental, and other. Retained all incumbents because there is no supported replacement across the relevant evidence contexts. No listening comparison is required for this PR because no selected recommendation changes.
